Drainage

A practical guide to keeping water away from Class 1 buildings.

01 · ATTENTION

Water damage often begins before water reaches the building.

Part 3.3 is not a collection of isolated dimensions. It is a coordinated defence that moves surface water, subsurface water and stormwater away from the building.

04 · APPLICATION

Subsoil drainage is a site response, not a default detail.

Where subsurface water needs to be diverted, the drain requires a uniform fall of at least 1:300, a maintainable outlet and a considered relationship with the soil and footings.

  • Confirm why the drain is required.
  • Provide a cleanable silt pit or sump arrangement.
  • Set the outlet at least 50 mm below the inlet invert.
  • Consider reactive clay and long-term moisture change.
MIN. FALL 1:300
