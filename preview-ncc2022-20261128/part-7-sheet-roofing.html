Keep the sheet roof draining

Profile, pitch, laps and flashings must work together. Water does not follow the product names on a purchase order.

Coverage: 7.1.1–7.1.2; 7.2.1–7.2.8

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 9

1. Confirm the roofing route

Part 7.2 is the metal-sheet roofing pathway subject to H1D7. It is not a blanket approval for every profile or roof shape. Identify the profile, base metal thickness, wind class and support layout before using the spacing and fixing provisions. Manufacturer information must describe the actual selected product.

Before applying these details, identify the relevant Volume Two pathway and read the state or territory schedule. Scope clauses identify the subjects covered; they do not make every detail applicable to every building. Use the defined terms and original figures where the provision depends on geometry.

7.1.1–7.1.2; 7.2.1. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 146. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Keep the product specification beside the roof plan.

2. Read water flow as a material connection

Compatibility applies to materials in contact and to water draining from one material onto another. Copper upstream of galvanised steel and lead upstream of aluminium/zinc-coated steel are examples that need attention. Lead is also excluded where runoff is collected for drinking. A small flashing can affect the roof below it.

Read with the source

7.2.2. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 147. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Trace runoff from every penetration and upper roof.

3. Pitch belongs to the profile

The minimum-pitch figure identifies different sheet profiles. A pitch suitable for one profile is not automatically suitable for corrugated or trapezoidal roofing. Check the actual installed pitch, including construction tolerances and deflection, against the nominated profile and pathway before setting out the supporting structure.

Read with the source

7.2.3. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 150. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record the selected profile and minimum-pitch evidence.

4. Supports and fasteners are a system

Sheet span and fixing selection depend on profile, thickness, wind classification and location on the roof. End spans and internal spans must not be confused. Check the table notes and the specified fastener arrangement before work is concealed; extra fasteners do not automatically validate an excessive support spacing.

Read with the source

7.2.4–7.2.5. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 150. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record end-span and internal-span measurements separately.

5. Lap in the direction of drainage

Full-length sheets from fascia to ridge are preferred by the provision where practicable. Where end laps are used, the specified minimum changes with pitch: 150 mm above 15 degrees and 200 mm between 5 and 15 degrees. The detail also addresses turning up sheet ends at ridges and valleys.

Read with the source

7.2.6. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 152. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check the applicable pitch range before measuring a lap.

6. Finish the water path

Flashings are formed from compatible material and arranged to drain out over the roofing. The provision specifies at least 75 mm flashing laps in the direction of flow and fastening near the lap. Penetrations must preserve drainage and any affected framing. At an eaves gutter, the sheet overhang detail is also part of collection.

Read with the source

7.2.7–7.2.8. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 153. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Inspect flashing laps and penetration drainage before access becomes difficult.

Practice: At a 10 degree roof pitch, two end laps each require 200 mm. What combined lap allowance is needed in mm?
Worked answer: 400. 200 + 200 = 400 mm. This is a material-length exercise, not a roof design.
