Choose the brick, mortar and tie together

A brick colour is a finish choice. Its strength, exposure rating and connections are different questions.

Coverage: 5.6.1–5.6.5: materials, joints, tie duty and placement

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 9

1. Start with the wall system

The components pathway is conditional on H1D5(6). Veneer and loadbearing masonry do not use identical unit-strength thresholds. For veneer, solid or cored units need at least 3 MPa and hollow units 10 MPa. Cavity and single-skin masonry use 5 MPa for solid or cored units and 10 MPa for hollow units. Identify the system before reading the product declaration.

Read with the source

5.6.1–5.6.2. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 90. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Keep the unit designation and strength evidence with the wall specification.

2. Exposure is a separate property

Protected, General Purpose and Exposure class describe resistance to environmental attack, not the colour or architectural quality of the unit. Exposure class covers severe conditions such as salt attack below a DPC, sea-front salt spray, heavy pollution and cyclic freezing. Mixing clay with concrete or calcium-silicate panels also introduces specified movement interfaces.

Read with the source

5.6.2(4)–(6). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 90. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record the exposure location as well as the selected product class.

3. Match the mortar pathway

Mortar must follow AS 3700 or AS 4773, or the volume proportions in Table 5.6.3. The table distinguishes general use from mixes suitable for concrete masonry. In the general-use column, Protected is 1:2:9, General Purpose 1:1:6 and Exposure class 1:0.5:4.5, in cement:lime:sand order. Additive and concrete-masonry notes remain part of the selection.

Read with the source

5.6.3. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 91. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Identify which column applies; do not substitute a familiar mix without checking the unit and exposure.

4. Joint appearance has limits

The nominal bed and perpend joint thickness is 10 mm unless otherwise specified. Raked joints are excluded in saline and heavy industrial airborne-pollution environments. Elsewhere, raking must meet the depth limits and preserve clearance to perforations or hollow cores. A desired recessed look cannot override the exposure restriction.

Read with the source

5.6.4. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 91. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Confirm the finish before the sample panel becomes the site standard.

5. Duty and spacing work together

Veneer ties need at least light duty through N2 and medium duty above N2. Cavity ties need light duty at N1 and medium above N1. In veneer, 450 mm stud centres pair with a 600 mm maximum vertical tie spacing; 600 mm stud centres pair with 400 mm vertically. Edge, top, bottom and articulation-joint placements need the separate location table.

Read with the source

5.6.5 and Tables 5.6.5a–c. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 91. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check the frame spacing and actual tie pattern, not just the packet description.

6. Protect the connection

Tie corrosion protection is selected from the exposure table. Severe coastal and industrial locations require the specified stainless steel or compliant engineered-polymer options. The table notes also require at least 50 mm embedment into each masonry leaf and installation that prevents moisture travelling along the tie into the inner leaf or frame.

Read with the source

Table 5.6.5d and table notes. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 91. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Photograph ties before concealment, with the product and exposure record.

Practice: A mortar batch uses 2 buckets of cement in the general-use 1:1:6 mix. How many buckets of sand?
Worked answer: 12. 2 × 6 = 12 buckets of sand. This is ratio practice, not a batching specification.
