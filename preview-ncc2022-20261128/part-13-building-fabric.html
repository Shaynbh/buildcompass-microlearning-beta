Keep the thermal envelope continuous

A bag of insulation has a rating. A building needs the right insulation in the right place, with bridges and gaps resolved.

Coverage: 13.1; 13.2.1–13.2.7

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 9

1. Establish the applicable energy route

Section 13 must be applied through Volume Two H6D2 and the jurisdiction schedules. NSW, NT, Tasmania and South Australia have relevant flags in the source. This lesson teaches the national 2022 provisions, not a statement that they apply unchanged to every current approval. Identify the building class, climate zone and assessment route first.

Before applying these details, identify the relevant Volume Two pathway and read the state or territory schedule. Scope clauses identify the subjects covered; they do not make every detail applicable to every building. Use the defined terms and original figures where the provision depends on geometry.

Read with the source

13.1.1–13.1.2; 13.1; 13.2.1. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 330. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Keep the approved energy assessment and applicable edition with the drawings.

2. Install insulation as a continuous layer

Required insulation must comply with AS/NZS 4859.1 and abut or overlap adjoining insulation as specified, including at supporting members. It must not compromise safe operation of services. Bulk insulation needs its intended position and thickness; compressing it into an undersized cavity can reduce the achieved performance.

Read with the source

13.2.2. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 331. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check hidden junctions and service clearances before lining.

3. Select roof and ceiling values together

Roof and ceiling tables depend on climate and the stated roof conditions. Metal framing introduces thermal-bridging requirements with defined alternative solutions. Loss of insulation around services also has a separate adjustment provision. Do not select an R-value from one favourable row while ignoring the roof colour, arrangement or framing assumptions.

Read with the source

13.2.3. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 332. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record the table, row and selected thermal-bridge treatment.

4. Coordinate roof lights with daylight

The national roof-light provision limits total area to 5% of the served floor area and sets combined system U-value and SHGC requirements. Shaft geometry affects the SHGC table. The daylight provision is a separate check: meeting a minimum light area does not remove the energy limits on the same opening.

Read with the source

13.2.4. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 345. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Use the same roof-light geometry in daylight and energy checks.

5. Read wall construction and bridges

Wall insulation selection distinguishes heavyweight and lightweight construction, climate and other table inputs. Metal-framed walls have thermal-bridging provisions and, in the specified direct-contact arrangement, a minimum R0.2 thermal break. Insulation outside the primary layer must also be coordinated with condensation and water-control requirements.

Read with the source

13.2.5. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 346. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check the complete wall build-up rather than a single insulation label.

6. Close the envelope at floors and garages

Floors over enclosed and unenclosed spaces use different tables. Slab edges, heated slabs and climate-specific slab insulation have separate conditions, including a waffle-pod exception in the stated clause. An attached Class 10a building must either achieve the required external-fabric performance or be separated by construction that does.

Read with the source

13.2.6–13.2.7. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 361. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Draw the continuous thermal boundary through the garage and slab junctions.

Practice: Under the national roof-light area rule, what is 5% of a 40 m² served room?
Worked answer: 2. 40 × 0.05 = 2 m². System performance, shaft geometry and the applicable assessment route remain separate.
