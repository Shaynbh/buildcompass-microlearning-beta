Follow the load through the brickwork

Veneer, cavity walls, single-leaf walls and piers have different jobs. Start with the load path before selecting a detail.

Coverage: 5.2.6; 5.3.1–5.3.7; 5.4.1–5.4.5; 5.5.1–5.5.6; 5.6.6; 5.7.6: selection and critical details

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 10

1. Identify the system

Cavity masonry has two structural leaves. The national prescriptive route limits overall wall height to 8.5 m and height between lateral supports to 3 m. Cross-walls or steel mullions provide wind support. These limits belong to this specific pathway under H1D5, not to every engineered masonry building.

Read with the source

5.3.1–5.3.3. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 78. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Mark the wall system and the proposed lateral supports on the plan.

2. Preserve the cavity and supports

Cavity walls use leaves at least 90 mm thick and a 35–75 mm cavity. Insulation and services must not reduce that cavity except for the stated mullion and vertical DPC exceptions. Cross-wall placement depends on openings and wind classification; Table 5.3.3 must be read with its support conditions.

Read with the source

5.3.3–5.3.4. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 78. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record clear cavity dimensions after insulation and services are allowed for.

3. Single-leaf walls need planned restraint

Single-leaf external walls rely on the specified piers, returns and roof connections. The tables separate height, thickness, roof loads and wind. Openings need support on both sides, and articulation joints must be coordinated with vertical supports. The special Class 10a route has its own building-width, wall-height and N2 wind limits.

Read with the source

5.4.1–5.4.5. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 81. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Select the correct table and figure before setting out piers.

4. Roof-supporting piers are tied down

The stated roof-supporting pier arrangement uses a minimum 290 mm square pier, maximum 2.7 m height and maximum 3 m centres. Roof bearing is at least 150 mm. Tiled roofs, sheet roofs and freestanding carports have different strap, rod and core requirements. A decorative brick column is not evidence of a continuous roof-to-footing tie.

Read with the source

5.5.1–5.5.5. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 87. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Identify the tie-down detail before the pier is built around it.

5. Subfloor piers have a different role

Engaged subfloor piers for the limited veneer arrangement have conditions including a single-storey building, roof span no more than 12 m, pier centres no more than 3 m and height no more than 1.2 m. Additional piers are required at openings and concentrated loads. Isolated subfloor piers instead use the size-and-height figure in 5.5.6.

Read with the source

5.2.6; 5.5.6. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 76. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Do not swap an isolated-pier detail for an engaged-pier arrangement.

6. Coordinate weather and connections

Masonry roof tie-down over an opening wider than 1200 mm needs specific design. Single-leaf weatherproofing also has a dedicated coating route in 5.7.6, including coverage at floor level and detailed window treatment. Clear water-repellent coating has a roof-overhang condition; it is not interchangeable with every paint system.

Read with the source

5.6.6; 5.7.6. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 94. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Keep connection, movement and weather details on the same coordinated drawing.

7. Carry opening and moisture details across the wall type

Cavity-wall openings use steel lintels complying with 5.6.7, except for the stated adequately supported openings not exceeding 500 mm. Damp-proof courses and flashings follow the referenced material and installation provisions in 5.7.3 and 5.7.4. Do not copy a veneer opening detail without checking the supported leaves and cavity-wall route.

Read with the source

5.3.5–5.3.6. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 79. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

Practice: A pier layout has four equal bays of 3 m. What is the total bay length in metres?
Worked answer: 12. 4 × 3 = 12 m. This layout calculation does not establish suitability of the pier system.
