Keep the installed services efficient

The equipment rating is only part of the story. Insulation, seals, controls and installation decide how the system operates.

Coverage: 13.7.1–13.7.9

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 9

1. Identify the service and its exposure

Part 13.7 applies through H6D2(2) to the stated building and associated pool classes, with jurisdiction variations flagged. Required insulation must withstand service temperatures and be protected from weather and sunlight. A product suitable inside a roof space is not automatically suitable exposed outdoors.

Read with the source

13.7.1–13.7.2. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 406. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record service type, location and protection requirements.

2. Insulate hydronic piping by location

Central-heating water piping outside conditioned space uses the material R-values for its particular location and climate. Internal enclosed routes, ventilated spaces and exposed routes have different requirements. The specified R-value is for the insulation, not automatically the combined pipe and air-film value.

Read with the source

13.7.3. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 406. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Map pipe routes before selecting one blanket insulation note.

3. Seal and insulate ductwork

Heating and cooling ducts need the specified air sealing and insulation, with separate values for ducts and fittings. Insulation must remain continuous and retain its thickness, and exposed or specified unconditioned locations need a protected outer sleeve. A draw band alone is not the stated flexible-duct seal without the accompanying sealing arrangement.

Read with the source

13.7.4. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 407. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Inspect joints and sleeves as well as the insulation label.

4. Control resistance heating by room

An electric-resistance heating system serving more than one room needs the specified isolation, temperature and time controls. The national provision also limits power density for living areas and bathrooms. Applying the bathroom allowance to all rooms would ignore the distinct room-use limits.

Read with the source

13.7.5. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 409. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Coordinate circuit and control schedules with room uses.

5. Check actual lighting power

The national base allowances are 5 W/m² in Class 1, 4 W/m² on the stated attached outdoor spaces and 3 W/m² in associated Class 10a. Use the proposed installation power, with control adjustments only under their defined method. Do not trade spare allowance between these categories. Jurisdiction variations must be checked.

Read with the source

13.7.6. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 409. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record luminaire power and the correct area category.

6. Coordinate water heating and pools

Water heaters follow the referenced Plumbing Code pathway. Pool and spa heating have listed energy-source options and conditions for covers, time switches and controls. Spas sharing pool circulation and standalone pump cases must be read carefully. Equipment selection does not remove the associated control and cover requirements.

On the job

Keep pool, spa and hot-water schedules with the building services review.

8. Check your understanding

1. Does an insulation R-value alone establish outdoor suitability?

2. Can every pipe route use the same value without checking location?

3. Does a nominal duct rating prove the installed joints are sealed?

4. Can one uncontrolled switch automatically satisfy a multi-room system?

5. Can spare garage allowance be transferred into the dwelling category?
