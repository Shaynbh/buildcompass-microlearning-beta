Make the room work after fit-out

Room labels do not create usable space. Heights, facilities, doors and light must still work with the actual finished layout.

Coverage: 10.3.1; 10.4.1–10.4.2; 10.5.1–10.5.2

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 9

1. Measure the required room height

The national minimum is 2.4 m for a habitable room other than a kitchen. Kitchens and the listed non-habitable spaces use 2.1 m. Stairs, ramps and landings use 2 m measured vertically from the specified surface or nosing line. A beam or bulkhead must be considered in the actual room geometry.

Read with the source

10.3.1. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 263. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check finished ceiling and floor levels.

2. Check sloping ceilings by area

Sloping ceilings have an area-based rule. The required height applies over at least two-thirds of the relevant floor area, with a separate habitable-attic value. Areas with a ceiling below 1.5 m are excluded when making this calculation. A single high point in the room is not enough.

Read with the source

10.3.1(1)(e)–(2). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 263. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Map the height bands on the floor plan.

3. Provide the required facilities

The facilities provisions address food preparation and washing, bathing, laundry, a closet pan and a washbasin. The arrangement includes conditions for laundry appliances and facilities located separately from the main dwelling. A room name on the plan is not evidence that the required facility and its usable connection are actually provided.

Read with the source

10.4.1. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 265. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check installed fixtures against the approved room functions.

4. Allow access to a sanitary compartment

A sanitary-compartment door must open outwards, slide or be readily removable from outside unless the specified clear space between the closet pan and doorway is available. The national dimension is 1.2 m, measured using the figure. This provision helps access if a person falls behind the door.

Read with the source

10.4.2. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 265. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check the actual pan and door swing before final hardware selection.

5. Calculate natural light from the right area

Natural light to a habitable room can use windows with light-transmitting area at least 10% of floor area, roof lights at least 3%, or the stated proportional combination. The openings must face the permitted external space. Count transmitting area rather than assuming the entire frame opening is glass.

Read with the source

10.5.1. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 267. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Identify the room floor area and the effective transmitting area.

6. Borrowed light has two checks

Borrowed light uses an opening between rooms and sufficient external light for their combined area under the clause. The adjoining room cannot be treated as an unlimited light source. Artificial lighting provisions apply to the listed spaces and interact with energy-efficiency requirements; providing a lamp does not automatically replace required habitable-room daylight.

Read with the source

10.5.1–10.5.2. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 267. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check both the internal opening and the combined external-light calculation.

2. Does a high ridge line alone establish compliance for a sloping room?

3. Does labelling an empty space laundry satisfy the facility requirement?

Practice: A 20 m² habitable room uses the window-only natural-light route. What minimum transmitting area in m² is 10%?
Worked answer: 2. 20 × 0.10 = 2 m². External-space, borrowed-light and other applicable conditions remain separate.
