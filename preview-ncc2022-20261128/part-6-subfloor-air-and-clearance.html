Give the subfloor a clear air path

An external vent is only the start. Air must pass through the subfloor without blocked corners or internal dead spaces.

Coverage: 6.1.1–6.1.2; 6.2.1

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 9

1. Know what Section 6 contains

Housing Provisions Section 6 contains subfloor ventilation and structural steel members. Timber framing, steel framing and structural software also have pathways in Volume Two H1D6. Part 6.2 applies to suspended floors and decks, including timber, steel and suspended concrete. Do not invent a timber-framing Part inside this Section.

Before applying these details, identify the relevant Volume Two pathway and read the state or territory schedule. Scope clauses identify the subjects covered; they do not make every detail applicable to every building. Use the defined terms and original figures where the provision depends on geometry.

Read with the source

6.1.1–6.1.2; 6.1.1. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 108. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record the structural framing route separately from ventilation.

2. Use the correct climate map

Subfloor humidity zones A, B and C are taken from Figure 6.2.1a. They are not the eight energy-efficiency climate zones. Without a ground membrane, the minimum aggregate opening areas per metre of wall are 2000, 4000 and 6000 mm² respectively. With the ground sealed by an impervious membrane, the corresponding values are 1000, 2000 and 3000 mm².

Read with the source

6.2.1(1) and Table 6.2.1a. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 109. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Confirm the humidity zone before calculating a vent schedule.

3. Provide a route through the walls

Clear debris and vegetation, grade the ground to the drainage provision and avoid dead air spaces. Distribute openings evenly where practicable, with openings no more than 600 mm in from corners. In double-leaf masonry, align openings in both leaves. Internal subfloor walls need unobstructed area equivalent to the adjacent external openings.

Read with the source

6.2.1(2)–(4). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 109. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Walk the route through internal walls, not just around the outside.

4. Measure to the lowest member

Ground clearance is measured to the underside of the lowest horizontal member. The table gives 150 mm where termite inspection or a management system is not required and 400 mm where inspection is required. Notes explain when the 400 mm requirement applies and a limited sloping-site reduction near external walls. Do not apply that reduction across the whole subfloor.

Read with the source

Table 6.2.1b. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 109. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Identify the termite system and the inspection route before selecting the clearance case.

5. Treat dampness as an additional condition

Excessively damp or frequently flooded subfloors need an additional measure beyond the preceding requirements. The listed alternatives include a 50% ventilation increase, an impervious ground membrane, or specified durable/treated timber or steel framing. They have material and application conditions. The clause does not excuse poor grading or a blocked air path.

Read with the source

6.2.1(5). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 109. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Resolve the source of water and the selected durability or ventilation response together.

6. Keep the opening free

Use unobstructed opening area when checking the airflow path. Grilles and later storage can reduce the effective opening. Where walls cannot be vented, the notes require additional measures to maintain the overall ventilation level. A blank rectangle on a drawing is not evidence of the installed open area or an agreed alternative arrangement.

Read with the source

6.2.1 and Table 6.2.1a notes. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 109. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Recheck the detail when access hatches, services or stored material change the path.

Practice: For a fictional 5 m wall in zone B without a ground membrane, what aggregate opening area is required in mm²?
Worked answer: 20000. 4000 × 5 = 20,000 mm². Distribution, internal walls and other conditions still apply.
