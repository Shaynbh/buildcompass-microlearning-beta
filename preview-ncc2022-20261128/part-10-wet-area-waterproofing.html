Build the waterproof layer before the finish

Tiles are the visible finish. Waterproofing succeeds at the joins, penetrations, edges and drain underneath.

Coverage: 10.1; 10.2.1–10.2.32: extent, materials and connected details

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 9

1. Map waterproof and water-resistant areas

Shower areas, baths, vessels and sanitary compartments have different required extents. Waterproof and water-resistant are different requirements. Floor substrate also matters: outside showers, timber-based floors have different treatment from the listed concrete or fibre-cement cases. Draw the required extents before choosing the membrane system.

Before applying these details, identify the relevant Volume Two pathway and read the state or territory schedule. Scope clauses identify the subjects covered; they do not make every detail applicable to every building. Use the defined terms and original figures where the provision depends on geometry.

Read with the source

10.1.1–10.1.2; 10.2.1–10.2.5. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 242. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Mark each wet-area type and substrate on the plan.

2. Select compatible materials

Waterproof and water-resistant systems depend on the specified material combinations. A water-resistant sheet is not automatically a complete waterproof wall. Membranes, substrates, sealants, primers and drainage components must form a compatible installed system. Product substitution needs to preserve the nominated performance rather than simply use another product of a similar colour.

Read with the source

10.2.6–10.2.11. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 248. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Keep the complete system specification and product records.

3. Coordinate levels, falls and shower type

Where a floor waste is installed, the national provision requires continuous falls between 1:80 and 1:50. Enclosed and unenclosed showers, hobs, step-downs and level thresholds use different details. A timber hob is not one of the listed hob materials. Plan the substrate, screed, tiles and waterstop together before setting drain heights.

Read with the source

10.2.12–10.2.20. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 250. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Dimension finished levels as well as substrate levels.

4. Keep the membrane continuous at movement

Substrates must be prepared and penetrations sealed using the applicable details. Bond breakers are required at the specified wall, floor, hob and movement junctions and must suit the membrane flexibility class. The shower membrane extent in 10.2.25 is measured from the finished tile level, so do not confuse it with an earlier substrate-based dimension.

Read with the source

10.2.21–10.2.28. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 257. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record junctions and membrane coverage before tiling.

5. Join the membrane to the drainage

The waterproof layer must terminate into or at a suitable drainage flange to create a waterproof connection under the applicable floor arrangement. Preformed shower bases also need drainage of the tile bed and a waterproof riser connection. A floor waste at the right height does not prove the hidden membrane-to-drain joint is complete.

Read with the source

10.2.29–10.2.30. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 261. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Inspect and photograph the flange connection before it is covered.

6. Finish doors and thresholds carefully

Perimeter and doorway waterstops must connect with the floor membrane and required flashings. Door-frame portions below tile level need the specified protection. These details must be coordinated with architraves and later fixing work so that the completed waterproof boundary is not punctured or bypassed by moisture-sensitive material.

Read with the source

10.2.24; 10.2.31–10.2.32. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 257. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Include the doorway in the pre-tiling inspection, not only the shower.

8. Check your understanding

1. Are water-resistant and waterproof interchangeable labels?

2. Does water-resistant plasterboard alone establish a waterproof shower wall?

3. Can the fall be checked only from the floor structure level?

4. Can one arbitrary bond-breaker detail be used for every membrane class?

Practice: At a fall of 1:80 over 1600 mm, what is the vertical fall in mm?
Worked answer: 20. 1600 ÷ 80 = 20 mm. Confirm the actual floor-waste provision and complete finished-floor geometry.
