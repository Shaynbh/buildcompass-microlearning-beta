Select the member and its connections

The steel section is one decision. Load width, restraint, bearing, connections and corrosion protection decide whether the whole arrangement works.

Coverage: 6.3.1–6.3.9

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 9

1. Use the right application

The structural-steel provisions operate within H1D6 and their stated limitations. Bearers, strutting beams, lintels and columns use different tables. A beam that looks similar to a table section is not sufficient evidence of the required steel grade, support conditions or load arrangement.

6.3.1–6.3.2. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 113. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record member designation, grade and function before looking up a span.

2. Bearer load width matters

Bearer tables distinguish single and continuous spans and timber and tiled floors. Effective spacing comes from the referenced framing pathway. The continuous-span provisions limit the difference between spans to 10%. Table notes describe the assumed loads and restraints, so the longest printed span cannot be used without those conditions.

Read with the source

6.3.3. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 113. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check the floor finish and both adjacent spans before ordering.

3. Strutting loads are positioned

Strutting-beam tables separate sheet and tiled roofs. Their point load must act within the middle third of the span. Effective load width, roof tie-down, connections and supporting columns remain part of the selection. Moving the strut towards an end changes the assumed arrangement even when the beam length stays the same.

Read with the source

6.3.4. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 116. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Dimension the strut position rather than relying on a diagram drawn to approximate scale.

4. Lintels need restraint

Steel lintel tables distinguish roof covering and effective load width. Loading is taken as evenly distributed and the top flange is laterally restrained at load points. Lintels used with masonry must also satisfy the relevant masonry pathway. A correct span with an unrestrained top flange is not the stated table case.

Read with the source

6.3.5. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 123. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Show lateral restraint and masonry interfaces with the section call-out.

5. Columns, bearing and connections

Columns are selected for supported area, height and roof or floor loads, and require restraint in two orthogonal directions. Member bearing is at least the member width at a single bearing and twice that width at an internal continuous support. Connection figures and specified corrosion protection for bolts remain part of the detail.

Read with the source

6.3.6–6.3.7. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 127. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check the complete path from supported member to footing.

6. Resolve holes and coating damage

Permitted penetrations are governed by the original member-zone figures. Do not drill a convenient hole based on a generic rule about the centre of a beam. Protective coatings depend on exposure, and cuts or welds require repair. Coordinate services before fabrication and retain the agreed structural and coating details.

On the job

Refer unplanned cuts to the responsible designer before work proceeds.

Practice: A 100 mm wide member uses the stated internal continuous-support bearing rule. What is the minimum bearing length in mm?
Worked answer: 200. 2 × 100 = 200 mm. All other table, support and connection conditions remain applicable.
