Keep sound paths out of the separating wall

A high-rated wall can be undermined by a gap, bridge or poorly coordinated service. The installed assembly is what matters.

Coverage: 10.7.1–10.7.8

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 9

1. Identify the separating wall

The national provision applies between Class 1 buildings and between a Class 1 building and an unassociated Class 10a building. It requires an airborne rating of Rw + Ctr at least 50. Northern Territory has a Part 10.7 variation. An internal bedroom partition within one dwelling is not automatically this separating-wall case.

Read with the source

10.7.1. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 274. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Mark the building boundary and association before specifying the wall.

2. Room adjacency can require discontinuity

Discontinuous construction is required where the specified bathroom, toilet, laundry or kitchen in one dwelling adjoins a habitable room other than a kitchen in the next. The room layout therefore affects the wall detail. A later kitchen relocation can introduce a requirement even if the separating wall position is unchanged.

Read with the source

10.7.1(1)(b). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 274. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check room uses on both sides of the wall.

3. Use a complete rated arrangement

The rating is established through the specified laboratory method or the listed wall configurations and construction provisions. Rw alone is not the stated Rw + Ctr criterion. Keep the full report or exact prescriptive configuration, including linings, studs, cavities and insulation, rather than selecting a rating from a marketing headline.

Read with the source

10.7.2. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 275. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record the complete assembly and its rating method.

4. Seal joints and avoid bridges

Masonry and panel joints require the specified solid filling, with the stated articulation exception. Double plasterboard layers have staggered joints and sealed interfaces. Timber noggings must not bridge the separate wall leaves. A rigid bridge can create a sound path even when both faces look complete.

Read with the source

10.7.3. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 275. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Inspect cavities and perimeter seals before closing the wall.

5. Coordinate services before installation

Services, recesses and penetrations must preserve the sound-insulating arrangement. The provision limits chasing and coordinates outlets and services with the wall construction. A fire-stopping detail and an acoustic detail may both be needed; satisfying one does not establish the other. Resolve the combined service arrangement before drilling or cutting.

Read with the source

10.7.4. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 277. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Keep acoustic and fire requirements on the service detail.

6. Carry the separation to its termination

A sound-insulating wall continues to the roof underside or to a ceiling that provides the required sound insulation. The listed masonry and framed configurations must be used as complete systems with their relevant construction conditions. An ordinary ceiling is not automatically an acoustic termination simply because it hides the cavity.

Check the wall head and any shared roof space.

Practice: A fictional tested assembly has Rw 56 and Ctr −6. What is Rw + Ctr?
Worked answer: 50. 56 + (−6) = 50. The complete assembly, scope and installation conditions still matter.
