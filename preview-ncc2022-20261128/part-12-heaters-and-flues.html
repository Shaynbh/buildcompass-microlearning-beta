Keep heat away from combustible construction

The appliance, hearth, chimney and nearby framing form one installation. A neat finish cannot make a missing clearance safe.

Coverage: 12.4.1–12.4.5

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 9

1. Choose the installation pathway

Part 12.4 provides the stated solid-fuel appliance arrangements, with the H7D5(a) alternative recognised by its application clause. Identify whether the installation is an open fireplace, insert or freestanding appliance. Do not mix clearances from unrelated tested products or assume a gas-appliance detail is the solid-fuel route.

Read with the source

12.4.1. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 322. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Keep the applicable installation standard and tested product instructions together.

2. Provide a non-combustible hearth

For the stated open fireplace, the hearth extends at least 300 mm in front and 150 mm each side of the opening. The upper surface must not slope away from the back hearth, and combustible material around or below the edge has a specified separation. Measure the complete section, not just the visible tile outline.

Read with the source

12.4.2(b). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 322. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check framing below the hearth before covering it.

3. Build the fireplace and chimney as specified

The open-fireplace wall arrangement uses specified masonry leaves, unit properties and footing support. The chimney lining has a specified mortar parging treatment. These are construction conditions, not merely decorative choices. A thin brick slip finish over a combustible backing is not the prescribed masonry fireplace assembly.

Read with the source

12.4.2(c)–(d); 12.4.3. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 322. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record concealed masonry and footing construction.

4. Terminate above nearby building surfaces

The chimney or flue terminates at least 300 mm above the highest building part within a horizontal distance of 3.6 m under this provision. Nearby roof changes can therefore alter the required termination. Read the original figure and the applicable installation pathway rather than measuring only above the immediate roof penetration.

Read with the source

12.4.3(c). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 323. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Review nearby ridges and additions as well as the penetration.

5. Install an insert with its tested flue

Insert fireplaces and flues must satisfy the specified AS/NZS 2918 testing and be fitted into the required masonry arrangement. The national detail includes a double-skin flue and 50 mm clearance between the outer flue and adjacent materials. Roof flashing, termination and hearth details still need coordination.

Read with the source

12.4.4. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 324. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check the actual flue system and surrounding concealed materials.

6. Treat freestanding heaters separately

Freestanding appliances use tested safety clearances or the stated alternative arrangements, including qualifying heat shields. The hearth and flue have their own conditions. A decorative panel is not automatically the required heat shield. Where the figures do not cover the installation, the referenced standard provides the route.

Read with the source

12.4.5. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 325. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Keep the heat-shield construction and clearances documented.

Practice: The highest relevant building part is at level 5400 mm. What minimum termination level follows by adding 300 mm?
