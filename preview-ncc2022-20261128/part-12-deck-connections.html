Connect the deck to structure

A ledger or waling plate must transfer loads into the supporting structure. Cladding is not a substitute for that connection.

Coverage: 12.3.1–12.3.4

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 11

1. Confirm the limited attachment route

Part 12.3 applies within H1D11 limits where a deck or balcony relies on the building wall for support. It requires a waling plate and limits the joist span nearest the wall to 3 m. A freestanding deck or a longer-span engineered arrangement needs its own applicable route.

Read with the source

12.3.1–12.3.2(a)–(b). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 317. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Identify the actual load-bearing wall member behind the cladding.

2. Choose the plate for its support

The national detail specifies different minimum timber plate sizes for core-filled masonry and timber framing, as well as a steel plate option. Material grade is part of the requirement. A plate that is wide enough for the screws is not automatically the specified structural section.

Read with the source

12.3.2(c). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 317. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record plate material, dimensions and grade.

3. Match spacing to construction

Fixing arrangements distinguish timber, steel and core-filled masonry supports and deck versus tiled-balcony construction. End and edge distances apply as well as maximum centres. The first fixing cannot simply be moved onto the end of the plate to achieve an attractive repeating pattern.

Read with the source

12.3.2(d). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 317. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Set out fixing centres with end and edge clearances visible.

4. Get the embedment into the right member

For the specified timber-frame route, No. 14 partial-thread self-drilling screws must embed at least 44 mm into the joist or bearer. Cladding and packing thickness do not count towards that embedment. Confirm the receiving member and its dimensions before selecting overall screw length.

Read with the source

12.3.2(e)(i). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 317. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Measure the non-structural layers separately from structural engagement.

5. Use the specified anchor evidence

Steel and core-filled masonry supports have specified bolt or anchor grades, washers and capacity requirements. For the masonry route, the stated working capacities include 2 kN in shear and 1.5 kN in tension. Do not transfer those assumptions to hollow veneer or an unidentified substrate.

Read with the source

12.3.2(e)(ii)–(iii). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 317. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Keep anchor data and substrate confirmation with the connection record.

6. Protect the connection and wall

Fixings within 200 m of breaking surf require stainless steel under the national clause; other areas have the listed corrosion-resistant options. The wall junction also needs a coordinated flashing detail. A structurally adequate connection can still cause damage if it directs water into the wall or uses unsuitable corrosion protection.

Read with the source

12.3.2(f) and figures. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 317. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Inspect flashing and fixing material before concealment.

7. Complete the wall flashing

Where cladding is removed to attach the waling plate, weather-exposed openings need the specified flashings at the top, bottom and sides. Flashing extends at least 150 mm beyond each side where practicable and drains outside. Joints overlap at least 75 mm in the direction of flow, with the specified fastenings and sealant.

Read with the source

12.3.3. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 319. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Inspect the water path before the plate and cladding hide it.

8. Brace the deck in its plane

Under the stated pathway, a deck more than 1 m above its supporting footing needs the specified diagonally opposed straps across the joists. Strap angles, maximum span, fixings and corrosion conditions apply. A deck extending more than 4 m from the wall needs the secondary strap arrangement. Ledger bolts do not replace this bracing.

Read with the source

12.3.4. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 320. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check bracing against the original figure and the actual deck projection.

10. Check your understanding

1. Does this detail automatically cover a 4 m joist span nearest the wall?

2. Can plate dimensions be selected only to fit the screw heads?

3. Does satisfying maximum centres erase minimum edge distances?

4. Does 44 mm of screw through cladding count as 44 mm structural embedment?

5. Can the core-filled masonry anchor detail be assumed suitable for hollow veneer?

6. Does correct bolt size make exposure protection optional?

7. Can a top-only sealant bead replace the specified top, bottom and side flashing arrangement?

8. Do ledger bolts alone establish the prescribed deck bracing?

Practice: A selected screw needs 44 mm structural embedment beyond 35 mm of plate and other intervening material. What minimum simple combined length is this in mm?
Worked answer: 79. 35 + 44 = 79 mm. Actual fastener selection must also satisfy the complete detail and product requirements.
