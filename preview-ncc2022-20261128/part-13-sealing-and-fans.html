NCC 2022 · PART 13 · REVIEW DRAFT

Seal unwanted leaks. Keep planned air paths.

Air sealing and ventilation are coordinated decisions. Close unintended gaps without closing the air paths that keep the building safe and usable.

Coverage: 13.4.1–13.4.7; 13.5.1–13.5.2

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 9

1. Check the sealing application

Part 13.4 applies through H6D2 and includes defined exceptions, including certain evaporatively cooled buildings and permanent ventilation needed for gas-appliance safety. Climate and jurisdiction matter. Do not seal a required combustion-air opening simply because it feels draughty.

Read with the source

13.4.1. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 399. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Identify safety ventilation before writing a sealing list.

2. Close an unused open-fire path

An open solid-fuel appliance chimney or flue needs a damper or flap that can close to seal it under this provision. This works alongside the fire-safety installation requirements in Part 12.4. The energy detail cannot override safe appliance operation or be improvised from an unsuitable combustible closure.

Read with the source

13.4.2. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 399. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Coordinate the damper with the compliant appliance and flue system.

3. Seal doors, windows and roof lights

The required sealing depends on conditioned-space use and the stated habitable-room climate conditions. Door bottoms use a draught-protection device; other edges use appropriate sealing, with the AS 2047 window exception. Roof lights have their own sealing options. A gap hidden behind trim is not automatically an effective seal.

Read with the source

13.4.3–13.4.4. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 399. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Inspect operation as well as the presence of a strip.

4. Seal fan and lining interfaces

Relevant exhaust fans need the specified sealing device, while ceiling, wall and floor junctions must minimise air leakage. Penetrations and frame interfaces are part of the thermal envelope. Coordinate seals with exhaust discharge and make-up air under Part 10.8 rather than closing every opening indiscriminately.

Read with the source

13.4.5–13.4.6. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 400. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check the envelope after services are installed.

5. Check evaporative-cooler dampers

An evaporative cooler serving a heated space or the specified habitable rooms in climate zones 4–8 needs a self-closing damper or equivalent. Its function is to limit unwanted air movement when the cooler is not operating. The application must be read with the earlier exceptions, not reduced to a universal rule for every climate.

Read with the source

13.4.7. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 401. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Confirm the damper arrangement and operating condition.

6. Choose fans by room and climate

The national ceiling-fan pathway operates through H6D2(1)(b), with jurisdiction variations flagged. Table 13.5.2 distinguishes bedrooms from other habitable rooms and uses room-area bands for number and diameter. A single small fan is not automatically the prescribed arrangement for a large living area.

Read with the source

13.5.1–13.5.2. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 402. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record room area, room type and the selected table entry.

Practice: For the national table example, a 32 m² non-bedroom habitable room in the applicable climate uses two 1400 mm fans. How many fans are required by that entry?
Worked answer: 2. The entry is 2 × 1400 mm. Confirm route, jurisdiction and installation conditions before applying it.
