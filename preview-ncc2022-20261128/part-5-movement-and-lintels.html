Leave room for movement. Support the opening.

A lintel and an articulation joint solve different problems. Follow both details when a wall or opening changes.

Coverage: 5.2.5; 5.3.7; 5.4.4; 5.6.7–5.6.8

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 9

1. Read the support route

Steel lintels follow this Part or H1D6(3). The prescriptive detail limits masonry above the opening to 3 m, requires at least three courses above it and keeps the long leg of an angle vertical. It also limits masonry overhang beyond the horizontal leg to 25 mm. A span-table entry is only one part of that arrangement.

Read with the source

5.6.7. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 96. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check orientation and the supported construction before comparing spans.

2. Bearing changes at one metre

A clear span no more than 1 m needs at least 100 mm bearing at each end. Above 1 m, the minimum is 150 mm at each end. Measure the clear opening rather than the overall steel length. These minimum bearings do not establish that the chosen section, masonry or support is otherwise suitable.

Read with the source

5.6.7(b)(v). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 96. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Show clear span and both bearings separately on a shop or site record.

3. Read both span columns

Table 5.6.7a separates openings with up to 600 mm of masonry above from those with more than 600 mm. For the 90 × 90 × 6 equal angle, the listed maxima are 3000 mm and 2650 mm respectively. The table specifies at least grade 300 MPa. Do not use the more favourable column after the height above the opening has changed.

Read with the source

Table 5.6.7a. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 96. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record the selected row, column and material grade together.

4. Coating is part of the lintel

The durability table links lintel class to protective material. Decorative coatings do not count towards its requirements. Cutting, welding or damage modifies the original protection and requires restoration to an equivalent level. A tidy paint touch-up is not automatically evidence that the original protective system has been restored.

Read with the source

Table 5.6.7b. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 96. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Resolve field cuts before installation and keep the repair specification.

5. Set out joints deliberately

The national articulation provision excludes walls on A or S soil classifications. For the stated other cases, joints are at least 10 mm wide. Continuous walls with small or no openings have a 6 m spacing limit plus corner conditions. Walls with openings more than 900 × 900 mm have a 5 m limit and joints within 1.2 m of openings. Height changes over 20%, thickness changes and material junctions introduce further triggers.

Read with the source

5.2.5; 5.3.7; 5.4.4; 5.6.8(1)–(3). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 76. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Use the complete layout rule, not a repeating joint spacing alone.

6. Keep movement joints able to move

Articulation joints must not be adjacent to arched openings. Flexible sealant is supported during installation by a compressible filler or purpose-made backer rod. The figures show different wall arrangements; a rigid mortar-filled gap is not the specified flexible joint. Coordinate tie placement at both sides without unintentionally locking the movement detail.

Read with the source

5.6.8(3)–(4). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 98. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Inspect the joint before sealant hides the backing and bridges.

1. Can an angle be rotated to hide its vertical leg?

2. For a 1.2 m clear opening, is 100 mm bearing the listed minimum?

3. Which listed maximum applies to this angle with 700 mm of masonry above?

4. Does decorative paint satisfy the durability table by itself?

Practice: A 1200 mm clear opening has 150 mm bearing at each end. What is the simple overall lintel length in millimetres?
Worked answer: 1500. 1200 + 150 + 150 = 1500 mm. Section selection and other requirements remain separate.
