Protect the edge and the opening

A barrier, a handrail and a window restrictor have different jobs. One does not automatically replace another.

Coverage: 11.3.1–11.3.8

National Housing Provisions text dated 1 May 2023. Confirm the approval edition, relevant Volume Two pathway, state or territory variations and referenced standards for the project. General education, not accredited CPD or individual compliance advice.

Section 1 of 9

1. Identify the fall trigger

A continuous barrier is required along the specified trafficable surfaces where a fall of 1 m or more is possible. Retaining walls have a particular access-path condition. Openable windows use their separate clauses. Read the operative wording carefully: exactly 1 m is included in the national barrier trigger.

Before applying these details, identify the relevant Volume Two pathway and read the state or territory schedule. Scope clauses identify the subjects covered; they do not make every detail applicable to every building. Use the defined terms and original figures where the provision depends on geometry.

Read with the source

11.3.1–11.3.2; 11.3.3. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 299. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Measure from the trafficable surface to the surface beneath.

2. Measure the right barrier height

The general minimum is 1 m above a landing, deck or similar level surface and 865 mm above stair nosings or a ramp. A limited short inside-landing exception and transition detail are provided. Do not apply the stair height across an entire balcony simply because the stair connects to it.

Read with the source

11.3.4(1)–(3). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 302. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Mark the change from flight to landing on the barrier detail.

3. Control openings and climbing

General barrier openings must not pass a 125 mm sphere. A face-mounted barrier has an additional maximum 40 mm horizontal gap to the trafficable edge. Where the possible fall exceeds 4 m, elements between 150 and 760 mm above the floor must not facilitate climbing. Strength and glazing checks still apply.

Read with the source

11.3.4(4)–(11). Open official NCC 2022 source, page 302. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check gaps, climbability and structural evidence independently.

4. Provide a usable handhold

Required handrails run along at least one side, with their top at least 865 mm above the specified surface, and remain continuous subject to the listed exceptions. The general exception includes a change in elevation under 1 m. A broad barrier cap is not automatically a usable handrail simply because it is at the right height.

Read with the source

11.3.5. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 304. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Check graspability and continuity at posts and changes in direction.

5. Treat wire barriers as tensioned systems

Wire-barrier tables depend on wire type, diameter, spacing, support spacing and tension or deflection. The unloaded gap alone is not enough because wires deflect. The explanatory notes identify the need to maintain tension over the service life. Use the complete table arrangement and keep commissioning measurements.

Read with the source

11.3.6. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 305. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record tension or deflection using the applicable table method.

6. Separate bedroom and other window rules

Bedroom protection is triggered where the floor is at least 2 m above the surface beneath, with further conditions when the opening starts below 1.7 m above the floor. Devices or screens must restrict a 125 mm sphere and resist 250 N. Removable arrangements add child-resistant release and barrier conditions. Other rooms use the separate 4 m trigger.

Read with the source

11.3.7–11.3.8. Open official NCC 2022 source, page 308. Use the complete clause, figure, table notes and scope together.

On the job

Record room use, fall height, opening height and hardware evidence.

Practice: A face-mounted barrier has a measured horizontal edge gap of 55 mm. How many mm above the 40 mm maximum is it?
Worked answer: 15. 55 − 40 = 15 mm. Check the actual measuring points and all other barrier requirements.
